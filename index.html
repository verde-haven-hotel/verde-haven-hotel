Helpful answers

Frequently asked questions.

Find quick answers to common questions about staying at Verde Haven Boutique Hotel.

How can I make a reservation?

You can submit your preferred dates, room type, number of guests, and contact information through the Reservation Inquiry form. The hotel can then be contacted to confirm availability and booking details.

What room types are available?

Verde Haven offers Standard, Deluxe, and Family Room options. Each room is designed to provide a comfortable and relaxing stay for different types of guests.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Standard check-in and check-out times should be confirmed directly with the hotel when making a reservation, as schedules may vary depending on availability and hotel policy.

What payment methods are accepted?

Available payment methods should be confirmed with the hotel before completing a reservation. Guests may contact the hotel through the phone number or email provided on this website.

Can I request a specific room?

Yes. Guests may indicate their preferred room type when submitting a reservation inquiry. Room requests are subject to availability.

How can I contact Verde Haven Boutique Hotel?

You may contact Verde Haven Boutique Hotel through 09915975630 or email us at verdehavenhotel@gmail.com . You may also visit our Facebook profile through the Social Media link above.

Where is Verde Haven Boutique Hotel located?

Verde Haven Boutique Hotel is located in Calapan City, Oriental Mindoro, Philippines.
